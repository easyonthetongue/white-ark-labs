# Oversized Weave Leather Tote

Oversized Weave Leather Tote reference developed for private-label customization and managed production.

Oversized Weave Leather Tote is a reference design for brands developing a custom everyday carryall with a wide-panel woven leather exterior, softly structured rectangular body, long shoulder handles and an understated leather tie closure. Proportions, leather grade, weave scale, edge finishing, lining, handle drop, colour, hardware and branding can be refined through sampling before production is coordinated with a suitable partner workshop.
